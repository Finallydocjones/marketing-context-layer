# AlumiPlate — Company Facts

AlumiPlate is a Minnesota company that electroplates high-purity aluminum coatings onto metal and composite parts for semiconductor, aerospace, defense, and other demanding applications.

## Origin of the technology

- AlumiPlate is built on the **Sigal process**, developed by Siemens and the Max Planck Institute over about three decades.
- In 1995, David Dayton acquired all worldwide rights and patents to the Sigal process. AlumiPlate then refined it for commercial use.
- Source: alumiplate.com/company/history

## What the company does

- **Service:** electrodeposition of pure aluminum coatings from enclosed, automated, solvent-based (non-aqueous) baths. Anodizing of the plated aluminum is also available.
- **Delivery model:** plating services from its Minnesota facility. Process licensing and IP transfer are available for customers who need on-site capability (FAQ).
- **Typical lead time:** most jobs are done in 2–3 weeks, with expedite options (FAQ).
