# NATO and International Defense Requirements

## What we know

- AlumiPlate's military/aerospace target market includes the **U.S. and NATO countries** (Fathom 2026-10-02).
- Gus said **NATO compliance is required** for international military work.

## What we don't know (OPEN-QUESTIONS #16)

"NATO compliance" could mean different things, for example NATO quality assurance requirements (the AQAP series), specific STANAG standards, or national defense specs in a given country. It also interacts with ITAR export rules for any non-U.S. work.

**Until Gus clarifies:**

- Don't claim NATO compliance, approval, or qualification in any content.
- Don't target international defense keywords or campaigns without approval.
- Treat international defense work as a later-phase market behind U.S. defense.
