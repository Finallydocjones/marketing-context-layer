# Test Methods Behind AlumiPlate's Performance Claims

ASTM standards are copyrighted. Name them and describe them in your own words, but don't reproduce their text. Official listings are at astm.org.

| Standard | What it tests (plain language) | AlumiPlate's reported result | Source of result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ASTM B117** | Neutral salt spray (fog): accelerated corrosion exposure in a chamber | 10 µm layer exceeds 1,000 hours; at least 2× MIL-DTL-83488 class minimums | FAQ; 83488 page |
| **ASTM G85** (SO2 annex) | Modified salt spray with sulfur dioxide: harsher, acidic industrial-atmosphere conditions | 336+ hours | FAQ |
| **ASTM B571** | Qualitative adhesion of metallic coatings | Used in addition to the spec's adhesion tests | 83488 page |
| **ASTM B487** | Coating thickness by microscopic cross-section | Thickness verification | 83488 page |
| **ASTM B499** | Coating thickness by magnetic method (nonmagnetic coatings on magnetic substrates) | Thickness verification | 83488 page |
| **ASTM B567** | Coating thickness by beta backscatter | Thickness verification | 83488 page |
| **GDMS** (method, not ASTM) | Glow Discharge Mass Spectrometry: trace-element purity analysis | Typically >99.99% Al | FAQ |
| **HCl bubble test** | Corrosion resistance of anodized surfaces (semiconductor) | Anodized pure Al beats anodized alloys; no figures published | Semiconductor page |

## How to cite results

- Always attribute: **"AlumiPlate reports…"** or **"In AlumiPlate testing per ASTM B117…"**
- Accelerated salt-spray hours **don't predict field life**. Don't convert hours into years of service.
- Don't compare hour figures across different test methods.
- Independent or OEM test data should be cited with the report name and date, once AlumiPlate provides it (OPEN-QUESTIONS #7).
