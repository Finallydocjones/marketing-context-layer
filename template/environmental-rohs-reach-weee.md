# EU Environmental Compliance — RoHS, REACH, WEEE

| Rule | What it is | Key limits relevant to coatings | AlumiPlate's claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RoHS** (Directive 2011/65/EU) | Restricts hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment | Cadmium ≤0.01%; hexavalent chromium, lead, mercury ≤0.1% by weight in homogeneous materials | "RoHS compliant" ✅ |
| **REACH** (Regulation (EC) 1907/2006) | EU chemicals regulation: registration, authorization (Annex XIV), restriction (Annex XVII) | Cadmium restrictions; Cr(VI) authorization moving toward restriction (see hexavalent-chromium) | "REACH compliant" ✅ |
| **WEEE** (Directive 2012/19/EU) | Collection, recycling, and recovery of **end-of-life** electrical/electronic equipment | No substance limits; it's about take-back and recycling | "WEEE compliant" ⚠️ |

## The WEEE wording problem

WEEE obligations fall on producers of electrical equipment, not on coating processes. Calling a coating "WEEE compliant" isn't really meaningful, and an engineer may notice. **Recommendation:** drop "WEEE" from new copy, or say "supports customers' RoHS and WEEE obligations". Confirm with Gus (OPEN-QUESTIONS #13).

## How to talk about it

- ✅ "Pure aluminum coating that is RoHS and REACH compliant: no cadmium, no hexavalent chromium in the as-plated coating"
- ✅ Note that post-treatments matter: a Type II hex-chrome topcoat would not meet RoHS limits. Specify trivalent or non-chromate treatment for RoHS applications.
- ❌ Don't say AlumiPlate makes a customer's finished product compliant. That depends on the whole product.
