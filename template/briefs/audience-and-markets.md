# AlumiPlate — Target Audience and Market Priorities

## Objective

Attract fewer, better leads. Per Gus, one good engineer or specifier lead is worth "100,000 clicks." Success is qualified leads in target markets, tracked in HubSpot. Raw traffic or form fills don't count.

## Single most important message

Pure aluminum solves hard engineering problems (corrosion, heat, plasma, toxic-coating replacement) that alloys, IVD, zinc-nickel, and cadmium can't.

## Audience (Gus's lead profiles, 2026-10-02)

| Rating | Who | Value | How to reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★★★★★ Specifiers | VPs, PhDs, design authorities with control over a product line | Highest; can make AlumiPlate a strategic supplier | Authority content, white papers, characterization data |
| ★★★★ Engineers | Technical problem-solvers researching corrosion, high-temp, or plasma issues | Primary target | Problem/solution content, gated technical resources, AI-answer visibility |
| ★ Buyers | Transactional; searching a spec (MIL-DTL-83488, QQ-P-416) | Low; quick orders, often lost to ISO/NADCAP requirements | Spec pages, but don't chase volume |

## Target markets (priority order)

1. **Semiconductor**: top priority
2. **Military / aerospace**: long-life and harsh-environment systems, U.S. and NATO countries. MIL-DTL-83488 is the key spec.
3. **Quantum computing**
4. **Thermal management**
5. **Renewable energy / batteries**

Emerging: **optics** (Gus to confirm as the November focus).

## Must never do

- Present MIL-DTL-5541 traffic or leads as growth or success
- Optimize for lead volume over lead quality
- Recommend markets AlumiPlate has said it doesn't want

## Success measures

- Qualified leads from target markets (HubSpot deal pipeline, with source and keyword attribution)
- An upward trajectory by year-end 2026, as proof for the 2027 plan
- AI-answer visibility for target engineering and defense prompts (Profound)
