# AlumiPlate — Q4 2026 SXO Brief

## Objective

Show measurable improvement in qualified conversions by year-end. The year-end goal was 30–50% conversion growth. Also build a credible, evidence-backed plan for AI/LLM-era search that sets up the 2027 plan.

## Single most important message

Technical foundations (schema, internal linking, authority content) are what make AlumiPlate visible to AI answer engines. Frame this as adapting to how engineers now search, not as a criticism of past work.

## Other decisions

- **Gated content:** approved (2026-10-08). R55 recommends what to gate, and AlumiPlate approves using red/yellow/green. Keep friction low and access immediate. Blogs stay ungated.
- **Blog:** deferred until after SEMICON West.
- **Corrosion pages:** optimize both overlapping pages now. Consider a merge in Q1 2027.
- **HubSpot:** enable the SEO/AEO tools. HubSpot is the source of truth for lead quality.
- **Paid:** handled in a separate ads roadmap (Microsoft Ads test; Google about $2,000/mo).

## Must never say or do

- Treat the quarterly plan as open to ad-hoc changes. Any change must document its impact on timeline and results (internal 2026-10-02).
- Schedule client meetings the week of October 12 (SEMICON West).
- Promote MIL-DTL-5541 (see policy).

## Success measures

- Qualified target-market leads trending upward (HubSpot)
- Pre/post results on priority pages (organic sessions, rankings, engagement)
- AI-answer visibility for target prompts, before and after schema and pillar content (Profound)
- Fewer orphaned pages and better crawl coverage (October vs. December crawl)
