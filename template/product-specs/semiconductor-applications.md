# AlumiPlate — Semiconductor Equipment Applications

Semiconductor is AlumiPlate's top-priority market (Fathom 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-08).

## What it is

High-purity electroplated aluminum, often anodized, for wafer-fab equipment hardware exposed to plasma, corrosive process gases, and heat.

## Components

Gas distribution plates; etch, ash, and deposition chamber components; other fab equipment hardware; solar manufacturing components.

## Why purity matters here

- No alloying elements, so no inclusions that act as contamination or corrosion initiators. This matters more as device nodes shrink below 5–7 nm.
- Compatible with fluorine and chlorine plasmas.
- Pure aluminum's high thermal conductivity helps spread heat from plasma recombination.
- Conformal plating reaches internal passages and complex geometries that line-of-sight coatings miss.
- It acts as a diffusion barrier against substrate element migration.

## Performance claims (AlumiPlate-reported)

- Anodized pure Al shows better corrosion resistance than anodized alloys in HCl bubble testing. No figures are published.
- Independent testing reportedly shows longer life for etch and deposition components. The source is a 2016 white paper linked from the page. Get the white paper before citing specifics (OPEN-QUESTIONS #7).
- Dielectric: >2,000 V per 0.001 in of anodic oxide.

## Limits

- MIL-DTL-83488 is not relevant to semiconductor. Don't use it as a proof point here.
- The aluminide coating rated to 1100 °C is **under development**. Don't present it as available.
- No outgassing data is published.
