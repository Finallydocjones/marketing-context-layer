# AlumiPlate — Approvals and Specifications

These are the specifications AlumiPlate's electrodeposited aluminum coating is listed against on alumiplate.com, as of 2026-10-13.

## Specifications listed on the website

| Specification | Issuer | Marketing priority | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIL-DTL-83488 (formerly MIL-C-83488) | U.S. Department of Defense | **High.** Primary spec for aerospace/defense buyers | Aluminum coating spec. Both IVD and electroplated aluminum can meet it. Aerospace/defense only; does not cover semiconductor or quantum. |
| MIL-DTL-5541 (formerly MIL-C-5541) | U.S. Department of Defense | **Do not promote** | Listed on the site, but AlumiPlate is de-emphasizing it. See policy. |
| F-078 | Raytheon Technologies | Neutral | List as on the site; not a customer endorsement |
| LGPS 1105 | UTC Aerospace Systems, Landing Gear Division | Neutral | List as on the site |
| LMA-PH010 | Lockheed Martin Aerospace | Neutral | List as on the site |
| Technology Approval Memorandum Ser 434300A/9.5112 | Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR) | Neutral | List as on the site |

## Related standards and claims

- **QQ-P-416 (cadmium plating):** AlumiPlate is positioned as a drop-in cadmium replacement. Buyers search this spec when looking for alternatives (Fathom 2026-10-02). The FAQ says the DoD has identified the coating as a preferred cadmium replacement. Confirm the document before quoting it (OPEN-QUESTIONS #5).
- **NAS4006:** the site has a "NAS4006 Superior Alternative" page title with no supporting body text found. Confirm before using (OPEN-QUESTIONS #6).
- **Test standards cited in the FAQ:** ASTM B117 salt fog (10 µm layer exceeds 1,000 hours) and ASTM G85 SO2 (336+ hours). These are AlumiPlate's stated results. Cite them as "AlumiPlate reports…".

## Wording rules

- The site uses both "certified to MIL-DTL-83488" and "complies with MIL-DTL-83488". Until that's resolved (OPEN-QUESTIONS #2), use **"complies with"**. The FAQ says AlumiPlate "can certify full compliance" per order.
- Aerospace OEM qualification: the FAQ says several aerospace OEMs have qualified the coating for flight-safety-critical components. Don't name which OEMs.
