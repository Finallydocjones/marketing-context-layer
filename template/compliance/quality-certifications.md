# Quality Certifications — ISO 9001, AS9100, NADCAP

| Certification | What it is | Issued by | AlumiPlate status (as of 2026-10-14) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ISO 9001** | General quality management system standard; current edition ISO 9001:2015 (a revision is in progress, so confirm the edition when citing) | Accredited third-party registrars | **Not certified.** Quality systems "based on" ISO 9001. Planned next year per Gus. |
| **AS9100** | Aerospace QMS: ISO 9001 plus aerospace requirements | IAQG / accredited registrars | **Not held.** No plans stated. |
| **NADCAP** | Special-process accreditation (e.g., chemical processing, which includes plating) used by aerospace primes | Performance Review Institute (PRI) | **Not accredited.** Planned next year per Gus. |

## Why it matters

Per Gus (2026-10-02), transactional spec buyers often require ISO or NADCAP. That's a key reason AlumiPlate loses 1-star buyer leads, and why marketing should target engineers and specifiers instead.

## Site wording problem

The FAQ says quality systems are based on **"ISO 9001/2000"**. That edition was replaced years ago (2008, then 2015). Recommend updating it to "based on ISO 9001" or removing it (OPEN-QUESTIONS #15).

## How to talk about it

- ✅ "Several aerospace OEMs have qualified AlumiPlate's coating for flight-safety-critical components" (FAQ; don't name the OEMs)
- ✅ "Quality systems based on ISO 9001", only if needed
- ❌ "ISO certified", "AS9100", "NADCAP accredited"
- When certification is achieved: update this file, `brand-facts/company-facts.md`, and the policy **the same day**. It's a major positive change for content and ads.
