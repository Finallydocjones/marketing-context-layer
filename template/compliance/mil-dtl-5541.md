# MIL-DTL-5541 — Chemical Conversion Coatings on Aluminum

## What it is

A U.S. DoD specification for **chemical conversion coatings** (often called chem film or chromate conversion) on aluminum and aluminum alloys. These thin treatments give corrosion protection and paint adhesion. The spec separates hexavalent-chromium coatings (Type I) from non-hexavalent ones (Type II), and corrosion-protection grades (Class 1A) from low-electrical-resistance grades (Class 3). Formerly designated MIL-C-5541.

*Confirm the current revision and the type/class definitions on ASSIST (quicksearch.dla.mil) before citing.*

## AlumiPlate's relationship

- AlumiPlate's coatings **comply with MIL-DTL-5541** when a conversion coating is applied as a **supplementary treatment** over electroplated aluminum (e.g., MIL-DTL-83488 Type II).
- AlumiPlate **does not want to pursue standalone commodity conversion-coating work.** Gus (2026-09-23): "We do not want to use any resources on MIL-DTL-5541." Leads from it are low quality: international buyers, chemistry requests, price shoppers.
